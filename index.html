4KB Qty from JSTERP, every 30 seconds

JSTERP only shows stock to someone signed in, so the numbers are read by a Chrome extension on the sync PC, OLT-0285. It runs by itself in a separate, minimized Chrome window and starts with Windows. No JSTERP tab is needed, and it serves everyone.

If this line turns red:

  1. Check OLT-0285 is on, awake and signed in to Windows. The sync Chrome reopens within a minute of being closed.
  2. If it says signed out, open the minimized Chrome window on OLT-0285 (the one with the Rack Planner button) and sign in to JSTERP there. The next run, within 30 seconds, picks it up.

It reads Zone Inventory with owner Shigetsu, Main Warehouse, start position 4KB, and adds each SKU’s locations together. A SKU with nothing there gets 0.

While OLT-0285 is away, any signed-in JSTERP tab can stand in: drag this to your bookmarks bar, open Zone Inventory, click it and leave the tab open. Rack Planner 4KB sync
