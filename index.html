4KB Qty from JSTERP, every 30 seconds

JSTERP only shows stock to someone signed in, so the numbers are read by a JSTERP tab that stays open — best on a PC that is always on, like the office PC. One tab is enough for everyone.

  1. Drag this button to your browser’s bookmarks bar (once): Rack Planner 4KB sync
  2. Sign in to JSTERP, then open Zone Inventory.
  3. Click the bookmark. A small box appears in the corner of JSTERP and the 4KB Qty here updates every 30 seconds. Leave that tab open.

It reads Zone Inventory with owner Shigetsu, Main Warehouse, start position 4KB, and adds each SKU’s locations together. A SKU with nothing there gets 0. If JSTERP signs you out, the line under the title here turns orange — sign in and click the bookmark again.
